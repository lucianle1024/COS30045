01 / Website design

A guided story, with room to explore

Read from national change to road-user differences, recorded event categories and age composition. The comparison years stay separate from the profile year. Desktop pairs short explanations with charts; mobile uses selected-group bars and exact tables.

Wireframes show information placement, reading order and narrow-screen alternatives. They are not screenshots of implemented pages. The long mobile drawing is shown large enough to inspect; use its SVG file for slide crops.

Why use a dumbbell, and what is its limitation?

Two positions on one count axis show direction and magnitude without duplicating bars. Year colour and shape stay fixed when the selected row changes. Grouped bars are more familiar; small differences can be hard to see in either view. We retain exact values and all 12 categories. Cyclists are not the only group to increase: pick-up/van occupants rose from 260 to 300 (+15.4%).

The 2019-2020 pair avoids crossing the known 2012 and 2017 recording breaks. It does not establish a cause or a change in exposure-adjusted risk. A brief full-series trend remains in the planned opening.

Grouping and alternative design

The compact sketch displays three literal categories and one remainder. The remainder includes Fixed or stationary object, Pedal cycle, Pedestrian or animal, Heavy transport vehicle or bus, 2- or 3- wheeled motor vehicle, Other non-motor vehicle and Train. A zero or absent aggregate contributes no width; all source labels remain in the exact-value table.

A row-normalised matrix is the alternative if readers need to compare many road users. The one-group Sankey is a candidate for following a total into categories; it still needs usability evaluation. Narrow screens use labelled share bars and a table. No links show a patient journey or fault.

These 2021 classifications describe the selected year's composition. They do not explain the 2019-2020 increase.

04 / Age composition

Which ages make up each road-user total?

Cyclists aged 40-64 account for 3,393 of 8,163 hospitalisations in 2021 (41.6%). The heatmap puts different group sizes on a common percentage scale, with count as an explicitly labelled alternative mode.

How to read the denominator and the colours

Each row sums to 100% before rounding. All age groups, including Unknown, contribute to that road user's denominator, with all source sex categories included. The percentage legend stays at 0-100 across years; count mode will use one dataset-wide count domain. Row selection uses an outline, so it does not change colour meaning.

Bars give a more precise single-group comparison and form the mobile default. Age bands have unequal widths. Composition is not population risk, and Unknown is not excluded to make the profile look cleaner.
